Camera consent

In live mode HUSH uses your webcam and the Presage SmartSpectra SDK to estimate pulse, breathing, blinking, talking and facial expression from video. These are used only as game signals: a racing pulse makes noise in the game. HUSH is a game; it is not a medical device and makes no health claims.

The camera opens only if you press the button below. HUSH itself stores, records and uploads nothing, and telemetry is disabled. Video processing is handled by the Presage SDK under Presage's privacy policy (https://api.physiology.presagetech.com/privacypolicy). Press Esc at any time to release the mouse and stop the camera.
